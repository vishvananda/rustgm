## 3. A′: the contract carries its owner's certified premises

### Rule

A member's conformance contract environment certifies its copies of the
owner's certified premises when:

1. the trait method contributes no clause of its own after elaboration
   (condition 1). The gate is structural: `contract()` counts the method's
   declared and elaborated inputs, `assumptions[method_bounds..]`, and the
   carry runs only when that count is zero. A method type parameter's
   implicit `Sized` is not a clause. It is the parameter's kind,
   `ParameterKind::Type { sized }` (`sema/declarations/generics.rs:231`), so
   such a method still carries (amendment 1, `cond-method-type-parameter`);
2. every trait clause of the contract is one of the owner's own by canonical
   value, and only the owner's certified ones are certified (condition 2).
   `Environment::opened_certificates` checks both: it returns nothing unless
   the owner holds every source, and otherwise only the certified ones.

A trait method's own clause could add a fact that normalizes one of the
premises' projections, which is why it disables the carry.
`cond-method-bound` (`Q: Copy`) stays stricter than rustc by name.

### Mechanism

`contract()` calls `carry_certificates` once, after it forms the
environment. `carried_certificates` returns the clauses to certify, and
`Environment::certify_normal_premises` records them. That call only
certifies an unshared environment, and the contract has just formed its
own. The relation then takes `equate_normal_premise` for the certified
premise, as the owner's ordinary query does, and the projection selects the
reverse `Iterator` impl.

The work is one step for the gate, plus three per contract trait clause
(collect, hold, filter), added to `body_work.canonical_steps` as
unbudgeted structural work, as `override_inputs.rs` does. The contract
already built the environment from the same clauses, so the carry is
linear in work the contract has paid.

Touched: `sema/traits/conformance/mapping.rs` (the carry),
`sema/traits/conformance.rs` (a `cfg(test)` re-export) and
`dev/tests/invariants/certified_premises.rs`.

### Invariants and bounded work

  carry on a store fixture. The owner has three certified premises, one of
  them quantified, and one uncertified premise. The contract holds the same
  clauses and a method type parameter.
  - Gated, the carry returns exactly the owner's three certified premises.
    The uncertified one is never certified.

### Record 1: a type-qualified call of a const trait's method

The site is `impl<Dyn: PointeeSized> Ord for DynMetadata<Dyn>`'s `cmp`
(`ptr/metadata.rs:251`): `<*const VTable>::cmp(&self.vtable_ptr(),
&other.vtable_ptr())`. Here `VTable` is an extern type, and the call
selects `impl<T: PointeeSized> Ord for *const T`
(`ptr/const_ptr.rs:1615`). The text comes from
`sema/declarations/implicit_methods.rs:85`: the type-qualified path query
found no applicable trait method. Core 1.98 declares `Ord` as
`pub const trait Ord: [const] Eq + [const] PartialOrd<Self> + PointeeSized`,
with `Eq`, `PartialEq`, `PartialOrd` and `Iterator` also const traits.

The probes (`repro/r1p/`) were declared in four steps, each written before
its round ran: `declared.txt` `f1a7aa41`, `declared-2.txt` `f1fdf9df`,
`declared-3.txt` `f42b85f5` and `declared-4.txt` `5027621e`. Their sources
are listed in `sources.sha256` (`aa489911`). They ran on P3 and rustc 1.98.0
(`tools/r1p-runs*.sh`).

- **Round 1 refuted the first hypothesis**, that an extern-type pointee is
  the cause (`round1.txt`). RustGM accepts a type-qualified trait method on
  a raw pointer to an extern type, in core's impl-inside-impl shape too. Its
  rustc column was void: the `std` programs collide with std's own
  `Ord::cmp` (E0034), and the `no_core` header lacked the `legacy_receiver`
  lang item. RustGM accepts those `std` programs where rustc reports E0034.
  That is recorded as observed, outside this record.
- **Rounds 2 and 3** (`out2-p3.log`, `out3-p3.log`, `round3.txt`) used
  `no_core` source-core crates with the `legacy_receiver` item. rustc
  accepts every shape below except where noted.

The reduction is a call to a method of a const trait whose supertraits are
`[const]` bounds, selected through a non-const impl for `*const T`. It fails
in the type-qualified form (`local_annotations`) and in the method-call form
(bodies). The same hierarchy as non-const traits resolves, and so does a
const trait without `[const]` supertraits. Neither the extern type,
`Iterator::cmp` nor the enclosing impl matters.

This is trait method lookup with const-trait supertraits. It is not an
impl-condition failure in the projection path, so D3's trigger for option C
does not fire. It is batch 20's lead, and it is not fixed here.
